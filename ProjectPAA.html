ABOUT THE PROJECT
Planet Air Aviation: A Digital Brand Refresh.

Planet Air Aviation is a charter flight company specializing in private and luxury travel. For this project, I was tasked with a comprehensive digital brand refresh to elevate their online presence and appeal to their high-end clientele.

(02)
PROJECT GOALS

I designed engaging and on-brand visuals for all social media platforms, creating a cohesive aesthetic that captured the luxury and professionalism of the brand. I produced a series of animated promotional videos and edited existing footage to create dynamic and polished content. This included motion graphics, visual effects, and sound design to tell the brand's story effectively.

(03)
RESULTS
What benefits our customer get from this project?

The result was a modern, sophisticated digital presence that accurately reflects Planet Air Aviation's commitment to excellence and luxury. This project allowed me to showcase my proficiency in delivering a complete visual package, from initial concept to final execution, driving brand recognition and engagement.
